How to read this page

Three snapshots establish a preliminary baseline. Membership change and Unit Metrics movement are reported separately. Metrics status: Green = 4–5, Yellow = 2–3, Red = 0–1 metrics met. Improving / Stable / Declining compares the selected endpoints; it is not a forecast or proof that commissioner contacts caused a change.

Youth counts come from Unit Metrics. Adult counts use the supplied council / district summaries; unit registrations may overlap, so unit adult counts are not summed to estimate unique council adults. Training and SYT are weighted by reported counts, not averaged unit percentages. Missing data displays as —. Units entering or leaving the dataset are not assumed to have opened or closed.
